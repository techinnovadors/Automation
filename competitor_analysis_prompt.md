# Competitor Analysis Prompt

You are tasked with performing a comprehensive competitor analysis for a given company. Please follow these steps to structure your analysis:

## 1. Company Overview and Core Business
- **Identify the target company's core business:** What do they do? What problems do they solve?
- **List their key products/services:** Be specific about their offerings.
- **Define their primary target audience/market:** Who are they selling to?
- **Identify their unique selling propositions (USPs):** What makes them different or better than alternatives?
- **Note any significant achievements, recognitions, or partnerships:** This helps establish credibility and market position.

## 2. Competitor Identification and Categorization
Based on the target company's core business and offerings, identify distinct categories of competitors. Consider:

- **Direct Competitors:** Companies offering similar products/services to the same target audience.
- **Indirect Competitors:** Companies offering alternative solutions to the same problem, or serving similar customers with different products/services.
- **Niche/Specialized Competitors:** Companies focusing on a specific segment of the market the target company also serves.
- **Broader Market Competitors:** Larger, more diversified companies that might have a small overlap with the target company's offerings.

For each category, list 3-5 specific examples of competitor companies.

## 3. SWOT Analysis (for the Target Company)
Perform a SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis specifically for the target company in the context of their competitive landscape.

- **Strengths:** Internal advantages (e.g., strong brand, unique tech, skilled team).
- **Weaknesses:** Internal limitations (e.g., small scale, limited resources, brand recognition).
- **Opportunities:** External factors that could benefit the company (e.g., market growth, new technologies, policy changes).
- **Threats:** External factors that could harm the company (e.g., intense competition, economic downturns, regulatory changes).

## 4. Competitive Strategies and Recommendations
Based on the competitor landscape and SWOT analysis, recommend 3-5 actionable competitive strategies for the target company. These strategies should aim to:

- Leverage their strengths.
- Address their weaknesses.
- Capitalize on opportunities.
- Mitigate threats.

Suggestions could include: market differentiation, pricing strategies, partnership opportunities, innovation focus, talent acquisition, etc.
